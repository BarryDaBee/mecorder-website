Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature works without paying. If MeCorder saves you time, you can contribute whatever you think it’s worth, including nothing.

Why pay at all?

Contributions fund the work: new blocks, better targets, more effects and the time it takes to keep a Mac app excellent.

Do I need an account?

No. MeCorder runs on your Mac and your recordings stay there.

What about the AI features?

The built-in offline interpreter is free and needs no network. Free-form requests can use your own Anthropic API key, which you pay Anthropic for directly.
