00 · The idea

A screen recorder sees pixels. MeCorder sees what you did: the button you pressed, the field you typed into, the moment it happened. So instead of editing every frame, you say what the video should do whenever it happens, and the edit writes itself.

01 · Record

Record the way you already work.

MeCorder captures the screen, and beside the pixels it keeps the cursor, every click, scroll and shortcut, so nothing is burned in.

02 · Understand

Every click knows what it hit.

Through macOS accessibility, a click is recorded as the element it landed on: a button called Create Project, a search field, the sidebar.

03 · Program

Say what should happen.

When Create Project is clicked: focus on it, wait a beat, highlight it, then reset. One Program, applied to every matching moment.

04 · Polish

It becomes an ordinary edit.

The Program compiles to camera moves and effects on the Timeline. Preview it, adjust any keyframe by hand, undo it in one step.

05 · Export

A video that feels designed.

What you previewed is exactly what exports, frame for frame. MP4, MOV or GIF, for YouTube, a social feed or a quick message.

Semantic targets

It understands what you clicked, not just where.

A screen recorder sees a click at a pixel. MeCorder also records what was there, so a Program can say Primary Button and keep meaning it when the window moves or the resolution changes.

  1. A click is recorded at a point.
  2. The point is resolved to the element under it.
  3. The name becomes something you can program.
  4. The layout changes. The name still matches.

Names come from macOS accessibility while recording. If an app exposes nothing, the click has no name and a Program skips it rather than guessing.

Programmable video

Your video can respond to what happens inside it.

A Program says when something happens in the recording and what the video should do about it. It runs on every matching moment, and compiles to camera moves and effects you can still edit by hand.

A small language for how videos behave.
 
Beyond single rules, Programs have the parts you'd expect from a real language, shaped as blocks.

IF Target matches Primary Button
Focus Primary Button
ELSE
Reset
END
 
 
Conditions
 
One Program can tell clicks apart: IF the target matches, do one thing, ELSE another.

PARALLEL
Focus Save
BRANCH
Play Sound
END

Start several things at the same moment, then carry on when every branch has finished.

IF clicks = 3
Highlight Next
END
 
 
Variables
 
Count, remember and compare. Later events see what earlier ones changed.

Two views, one edit
 
Use intent when you can. Use the Timeline when you need it.
 
    
 
 
Timeline: edit what happened. Clips, scenes, camera keyframes, text, effects and audio, laid out in time and adjustable to the frame.
 
Program: define what should happen. The rule behind those keyframes, written once and applied to every moment that matches.
 
 
Program doesn't replace the Timeline. It writes to it: every block becomes ordinary keyframes and effects that you can open, move or delete.
