MeCorder records your Mac's screen and turns the recording into a finished video. It knows what you clicked, typed and scrolled, so it can zoom into the right places on its own, and it lets you describe what the video should do whenever something happens. Everything it does for you stays editable, and your recordings never leave your Mac.

A MeCorder video goes through five stages:

1. **Record** your screen, voice, webcam and system audio, each as its own track.
2. **Understand** what happened: every click, hover, scroll and key press becomes a named event, such as *Click Save*.
3. **Program** what the video should do: *when* something happens, *do* these camera moves and effects.
4. **Polish** anything by hand on the Timeline: cuts, camera, cursor, text, effects and audio.
5. **Export** an MP4, MOV or GIF that looks exactly like the preview.
